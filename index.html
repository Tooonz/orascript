About Us

We are Havish Mistry and Paavani Iyer, two students who noticed how much paper one class fills with handwritten notes every day, and how hard note-taking can be for classmates with disabilities.

OraScript started as a Python speech-to-text program for our science fair. It now runs as a full classroom tool with a teacher portal and a local AI tutor for students.

🎯

Our mission

Every lesson accessible to everyone, with zero paper wasted.
🧠

Our approach

Speech recognition for teachers and a small AI tutor for students.
🌍

Our goal

A greener classroom and a smaller carbon footprint.

Why OraScript?

🌱

Sustainable

One class of 43 students using 10 pages a day uses 430 pages daily. Digital notes remove that waste.
♿

Accessible

Live text supports students who are deaf, hard of hearing, or unable to write quickly.
⚡

Effortless

The teacher just speaks. Notes are ready to edit, copy, save and share.
🌐

Multilingual

🔒

Private
